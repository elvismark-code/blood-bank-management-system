## Database
- Schema: blood_bank_db (MySQL, JDBC port 3306)
- Driver: mysql-connector-j-26.7.0.jar (add the .jar, not the folder)
- DB password: kept in DBConnection.java only

## Exact enum values
- appointment.status: Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled, No-show
- staff.role: Doctor, Nurse, Lab Technician, Receptionist, Manager
- user_account.access_level: Admin, Staff
- blood_unit.component: Whole Blood, Plasma, Platelets, Red Cells
- blood_unit.status: Quarantine, Available, Reserved, Issued, Expired, Discarded
- blood_request.urgency: Normal, Urgent, Emergency
- blood_request.status: Pending, Approved, Fulfilled, Rejected
- blood_test.test_name: HIV, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, Syphilis, Malaria
- blood_test.result: Negative, Positive
- gender (donor, patient): Male, Female, Other

## Insert order
blood_bank, blood_group, hospital → staff, donation_camp → user_account, donor, patient
→ appointment, health_screening → donation → blood_unit → blood_test
→ blood_request → blood_issue
